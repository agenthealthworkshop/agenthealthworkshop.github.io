About

AgentHealth brings together machine learning, clinical, and biomedical researchers to study agents that retrieve evidence, use tools, and make multi-step decisions. We focus on grounding, realistic evaluation, and expert oversight across clinical care and biomedical discovery.

Submission topics

  • Clinical workflows and patient-facing agents
  • Biomedical and scientific discovery
  • Knowledge, memory, and multimodal reasoning
  • Evaluation, safety, privacy, and public health

Submission guidelines

  • Double-blind review on OpenReview, with at least two human reviews per paper.
  • New and ongoing work is welcome. Papers already published at major ML conferences are out of scope; concurrent submissions must follow both venues’ policies.
  • Papers must be primarily human-authored, with AI assistance disclosed. AI-generated short papers and protected health information are not permitted.
  • Accepted papers will be public on OpenReview. Authors present in person; exceptional travel constraints may be accommodated with a recording or a poster presented on their behalf.

TimeSession

Invited talks include 20 minutes of presentation and 5 minutes of discussion. Posters, the case clinic, and the panel provide 190 minutes of direct exchange.

Agent Case Clinic 60 min

Inspect one clinical and one biomedical discovery trajectory in small groups. Identify unsupported steps and where expert oversight is needed. Only synthetic or public data will be used; cases, answer keys, and a failure taxonomy will be published within two weeks.

Discussion panel 40 min

What will it take to trust agents in the clinic and the lab? We plan three or four external panelists, including a practicing clinician, with an organizer moderating. Panelists will be announced once confirmed.
