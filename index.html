About

AgentHealth brings together machine learning, clinical, and biomedical researchers to study agents that retrieve evidence, use tools, and make multi-step decisions. We focus on grounding, realistic evaluation, and expert oversight across clinical care and biomedical discovery.

  • Clinical workflows and patient-facing agents
  • Biomedical and scientific discovery
  • Knowledge, memory, and multimodal reasoning
  • Evaluation, safety, privacy, and public health

Submission policies

We welcome methods, systems, benchmarks, negative results, analyses, and position papers. Short papers may present work in progress, modest results, re-analyses, dataset notes, or case studies. Students and researchers new to ML venues are welcome.

Each paper receives at least two human reviews assessing relevance, soundness, evaluation validity, limitations, and clarity. Work already published at major ML conferences is out of scope; concurrent submission is permitted where the other venue allows it.

Papers must be primarily human-authored, with AI assistance disclosed. AI-generated short papers are not permitted. Submissions must not contain protected health information. Accepted papers will be public on OpenReview.

At least one author is expected to present in person. Exceptional travel constraints may be accommodated with a recording or a poster displayed on the author's behalf.

Agent Case Clinic & discussion panel

The case clinic brings domain experts and ML researchers together to inspect two agent trajectories: one clinical case and one biomedical discovery case. Groups identify unsupported steps and decide where expert oversight is needed. Only synthetic or publicly released data will be used; cases, answer keys, and the resulting failure taxonomy will be published within two weeks.

Panel: What will it take to trust agents in the clinic and the lab? We plan three or four external panelists, including a practicing clinician, with an organizer moderating. Panelists will be announced once confirmed.
